- **HOLOS 88 website**: canonical museum, full archive, browsing experience, public identity.

## Reader flow

SNS / search → HOLOS article → free Substack letter → Patreon paid issue or product → HOLOS archive and Life Specimen.

## Rules

1. Never make Substack the only copy of an article.
2. Keep editable masters inside the HOLOS content archive.
3. Do not auto-add a visitor to both services without explicit consent.
4. Explain clearly when a link leaves HOLOS for Substack or Patreon.
5. Export the Substack subscriber CSV regularly and keep a dated backup.
6. Start Patreon with few tiers; add support obligations only after workload is measured.
