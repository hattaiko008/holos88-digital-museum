HOLOS 88 has no prescribed route.

Choose SHE CREATES when changes in body and season feel close. Choose WATCH THE NOW when the news feels too distant from ordinary life. Enter ECHO and the Museum when you want to stay with a question through psychology, history, or philosophy.

The opening editions of SHE CREATES and WATCH THE NOW are currently available as free full-length samples.

Our first paid publication will pair a deeper seasonal edition with a printable seven-day observation notebook. It will add something readers can use and revisit, rather than repackaging the same free article.

This is a free public post. The contents, sample pages, and price will be shown before the first release.
