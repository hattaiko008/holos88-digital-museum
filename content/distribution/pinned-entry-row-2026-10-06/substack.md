## Where Would You Like to Begin?

HOLOS 88 has no prescribed route.

Choose SHE CREATES when changes in your body or the season feel close.

Choose WATCH THE NOW when the numbers in the news seem far removed from everyday life.

Enter ECHO and the Museum when you want to stay with a question through psychology, history, or philosophy without rushing toward an answer.

You do not need to read everything from the beginning. Choose the window that feels closest to your life today.

The opening editions of SHE CREATES and WATCH THE NOW are currently available as free full-length samples.
