# A LETTER FROM TEAM HOLOS

## Internal role

SATORI’S VIEW remains the internal editorial role. The public expression is:

**A LETTER FROM TEAM HOLOS**  
**読み終えた、あなたへ。**

Public signature:

**TEAM HOLOS**

Do not use 「TEAM HOLOSより」.

## Purpose

The letter is not a summary, conclusion, lesson, encouragement, or final piece needed to complete an article. The article is already complete.

Read each finished article freshly. Do not force it back into the general philosophy of HOLOS 88. Look for what remains after reading, and open one small adjacent window.

Possible forms:

- one question
- a short letter with room left around it
- a small observation that can return to daily life
- a landscape or image that remains
- a single line when more words would weaken it

The tone is quiet, intelligent, and warm. Avoid inflated mysticism, over-explanation, and sweet reassurance. Do not decide what the reader should feel.

Each subject has its own exit:

- 種には、種の出口がある。
- 馬には、馬の出口がある。
- 市場には、その朝だけの出口がある。

## New museum principle

HOLOS 88 now collects three things:

**OBJECT → RELATIONSHIP → ENCOUNTER**  
**もの → 関係 → 出会い**

ENCOUNTER is the reader’s lived time with the Museum:

- 私はこの窓を見た。
- ここで道草した。
- これを本棚へ残した。
- しばらくして、別の窓に出会った。

The Museum continues outside the screen when something in Life looks slightly different after reading.

## Workflow

1. CHATO completes editorial review, source checks, and layout readiness.
2. The finished article is passed to SATORI without a requested conclusion.
3. SATORI reads the article from a blank state and writes one adjacent window.
4. CHATO checks only factual claims, repetition, and fit with the article. Preserve the letter’s breathing room.
5. Publish at the article exit under **A LETTER FROM TEAM HOLOS** and sign **TEAM HOLOS**.
