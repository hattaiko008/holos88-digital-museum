# HOLOS 88｜MONETIZATION & DISTRIBUTION 01

Date: 2026-09-20  
Status: strategy draft / no account or payment changes

## Core principle

HOLOSは商品を押し売りする場所ではなく、読み物・標本・図版・道草の質によって「もっと読みたい」「この視点で相談したい」と思ってもらう。収益化は入口ではなく、信頼が積み上がった後の自然な出口に置く。

## Channel roles

| Channel | Role | Main material | Return path |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOLOS 88 | home museum / owned archive | full articles, specimens, relationships | email / free reading list |
| note | Japanese discovery and short essays | hachico essays, excerpts, seasonal windows | HOLOS article or email |
| Substack | optional publication and email relationship | ECHO essays, monthly letter, audio/video essays | free list → paid later |
| Instagram | visual field notes | one image, detail crop, short caption, process | article / save / profile |
| X | live thinking and links | observation, source note, conversation | article / newsletter |
| Pinterest | long-tail visual discovery | woodcuts, diagrams, seasonal cards, article covers | article landing page |
| YouTube | screen essays | article with the time of an exhibition | HOLOS / newsletter |

## Monetization ladder

1. **Open** — public articles, specimens, source windows, short social excerpts.
2. **Remember** — free email letter / seasonal index / new article notice.
3. **Support** — optional paid membership or monthly letter after a stable free archive exists.
4. **Deepen** — digital magazine, screen essay collection, reading circle, small online salon.
5. **Commission** — editorial direction, research, brand/world-building, cultural curation and consulting. This should arrive through demonstrated work, not aggressive sales copy.

Substack's free publishing is possible, but paid subscriptions use Stripe; therefore it cannot currently be treated as the only paid route. note's official membership page states that memberships can be combined with single paid articles and lists a 10% fee, so it is a candidate for the Japanese paid layer. Confirm current payment availability and fees at setup time.

## Content reuse rule

One finished HOLOS article becomes a small constellation, not seven copies:

`article → 1 visual pin → 1 Instagram image → 1 short X observation → 1 note excerpt → 1 newsletter window → optional screen essay`

Each adaptation changes its purpose and length. No automatic cross-posting without editorial review. Scientific, political, financial and historical claims retain their source link.

## Pinterest direction

Pinterest fits HOLOS because it is a visual discovery and save-oriented channel. Use high-resolution archive material, strong framing, restrained typography and a clear link to a related landing page. Do not turn it into a catalogue of products. The first boards can be `SEASONAL WINDOWS`, `NATURAL HISTORY`, `MUSEUM OF RELATIONSHIPS`, `OLD BOOKS / NEW QUESTIONS`.

## Automation boundary

Automate drafts, resizing, metadata templates, scheduling suggestions, link checks and monthly performance summaries. Keep human approval for public posts, political/current-affairs claims, paid offers, customer replies, image rights, and anything that speaks in hachico or ECHO's public voice.

## Next reversible step

Before opening paid services, prepare a 30-day content map using three finished articles: one hachico LIFE NOTE, one ECHO/Museum article, and one seasonal window. Measure saves, clicks, completed reads, email sign-ups and replies. Do not optimize for follower count alone.
