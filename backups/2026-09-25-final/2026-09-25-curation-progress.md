### Editorial rule for WATCH THE NOW

A viewpoint may be stated. Facts, interpretation, and inference must remain distinguishable. Explain materially different positions without caricature, then examine the event through life, relationship, dignity, ecology, and the people or beings excluded by the headline or number.

### Cost-control decision

Keep model routing simple inside Codex:

- Luna low: small mechanical edits, searches, inventory, formatting, synchronization.
- Luna medium: clear briefs and coordinated routine updates.
- Sol medium/high: writing, research, fact checks, and implementation requiring judgment.
- Astra: broad architecture, ambiguous synthesis, difficult visual/editorial decisions, or recovery after repeated failure.
- Deterministic tools establish build, test, link, diff, and file truth.

Do not add the external Jev service now. Reconsider only after measured task logs show that routing decisions themselves create meaningful cost.

### Internationalization direction

Build HOLOS 88 as a bilingual Japanese/English Museum first.

- A visible `JP / EN` switch should change the complete interface and article language.
- Preserve Japanese as the editorial source language.
- Store English as an edited publication version, not an uncontrolled browser translation.
- Keep URLs and content structure ready for future languages.
- Recommended URL structure: `/jp/...` and `/en/...`, with the language choice remembered on the visitor’s device.
- Shared assets, relationships, citations, rights information, and article IDs should remain language-neutral.
- Translate interface labels once through a shared dictionary; translate article bodies as separate editorial content.
- Do not launch many machine-translated languages at once. Add languages only when editorial review is possible.

Potential later languages: French, Spanish, Chinese, and Korean. Final priority should follow actual readership and available editorial review.
