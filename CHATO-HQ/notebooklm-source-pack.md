# HOLOS88｜CHATO research context

## Identity

HOLOS88 is a digital museum and editorial project that connects news, seasons, bodies, culture, history, tools, and ways of living. Its editorial stance values careful observation, source distinction, readable depth, and practical judgement.

## Editorial structure

- HP is the canonical home and archive.
- note supports discovery and Japanese readership.
- Substack supports subscriptions and letters.
- Social platforms introduce fragments and lead readers inward.
- Free articles offer a useful angle; paid editions deepen the classical, scientific, medical, historical, and cultural context.

## Research rules

- Distinguish fact, interpretation, hypothesis, and uncertainty.
- Do not invent citations, images, provenance, or historical facts.
- Preserve creator, title, date, institution, source, licence, credit, and alt information.
- Keep medical and nutritional information separate from editorial reflection.
- Use NotebookLM to compare supplied sources and locate tensions, omissions, chronology, and possible questions. Return conclusions to the project files after human review.

## Initial research themes

1. Whole Earth Catalog and the politics of tools, access, commons, platforms, and repair.
2. Seasonal food, nutrition, the body, medication, and food culture.
3. Calendars, astronomy, cultural symbols, and everyday judgement.
4. Publishing history, magazine design, and image-text relationships.
5. Audio and video adaptations of HOLOS88 articles.
