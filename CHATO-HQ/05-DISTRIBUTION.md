# DISTRIBUTION

## Primary flow

SNS → note / Substack → HP → patron or support route

## Channels

- HP: canonical article and archive
- note: discovery and Japanese readership
- Substack: subscription and letter delivery
- Newsletter: compact preview with article links
- Instagram / Threads / Pinterest / X: discovery and excerpts
- LinkedIn: professional and international context
- lit.link: single-link entry point
- Followr: assisted scheduling after destination tests

## Publish checklist

- Canonical URL
- Title / deck / excerpt
- Image sizes and safe areas
- Alt text and credits
- UTM links
- Disclosure for affiliate links
- Mobile preview
- Link check
- Archive copy
