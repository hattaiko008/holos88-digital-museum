## Editorial voice

Preserve the approved Japanese rewrite brief for seasonal food, nutrition, body, medication, and food-culture articles. Do not thin specialist content into generic wellness copy. Separate established information, interpretation, and editorial reflection.

## Article design standard

Long-form articles use `content/briefs/article-magazine-format-01.md` as the shared editorial and visual standard. Apply its rhythm, captions, question devices, source distinctions, responsive checks, and collection-shelf links during Edit → Design. Adapt the composition to the subject; do not duplicate one article's decoration mechanically.
