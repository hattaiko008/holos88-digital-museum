# MEDIA PIPELINE

## Audio

- Concept
- Script
- Voice / speaker permission
- Music and sound rights
- Recording
- Edit and noise check
- Transcript
- Cover image
- Description and links
- Publish and archive

## Video

- Concept and audience
- Script / storyboard
- Shot list and asset list
- Image, footage, font, music rights
- Recording / generation
- Edit
- Captions and transcript
- Thumbnail and vertical cut
- Description, credits, related article
- Publish and archive

## Master record

Every media item should retain: title, version, date, duration, aspect ratio, master-file path, source assets, rights, transcript, captions, thumbnail, destination URLs, and repurposed clips.
